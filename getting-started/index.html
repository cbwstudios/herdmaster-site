Getting Started

Set up your herd in about five minutes.

A short guide to getting HerdMaster running, from installing the app to importing your records and keeping up with the daily work. No prior setup needed.

~5 minutes5 stepsFree to start, no credit card

Stuck? FAQ or Contact.
1

Install the app

At launch, HerdMaster is free on the App Store. HerdMaster runs on iPhone and iPad. Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later. First launch is quick: create an account, confirm your email, and you land on an empty herd ready for your first animals.

Free to startNo credit card and no commitment. The free Hobby tier covers up to 10 active herd animals with full breeding records, so you can try the whole app before deciding.
2

Add or import your herd

However your records live now, there's a path in. Pick the one that fits, you can mix and match later.

Start fresh

Add animals by hand and link pedigrees as you go. Best for a small herd or a clean start.

From Evans

Export your Evans XML and import animals, winnings, and litters in one step. Evans XML is the primary and recommended import path. Evans HTM and Evans text-based PDF pedigree import are fallbacks for older exports when XML is unavailable. PDF import supports text-based PDFs only, not scanned or image-only PDFs.

From Everbreed

Everbreed Animals CSV and Litters CSV exports, or a public Everbreed pedigree link. Both CSV imports require a Breeder or Show Breeder plan.

To add an animal by hand:

Herd › Add animal

To import an Evans export:

Settings › Import › Evans
Imports match incoming animals to existing records, let you review conflicts before saving, and roll back if a save fails.
Heads up: choose Beta/Other in EvansDon't pick Show Tails or All ACTIVE animals. Show Tails only exports full pedigrees for active animals. To include archived animals, choose Beta/Other, All animals (both Active and Archived), All Breeds, and All Other Items.
3

Build your pedigrees

Set each animal's sire and dam and HerdMaster assembles pedigrees showing the animal plus three ancestor generations. Scroll it full-screen to check your lines, see where a pedigree is complete or thin, and export a clean PDF to print or hand to a buyer. Imported animals arrive with their trees filled from the export, so there's nothing to retype.

Animal › Edit › Sire / Dam
Export a PDFOpen any animal's pedigree and choose Share › PDF to print it or send it to a buyer.
4

Track the daily work

Once your herd is in, HerdMaster keeps the day running. Most of this happens in a few taps, right from the barn.

  • Record breedings. Log a pairing in seconds and HerdMaster tracks the timeline, so you know when to expect the next litter.
  • Log litters and kit weights. Record each litter as it's born, then track weights over time to spot your strongest growers early.
  • Set up health records. Keep weights, treatments, and notes attached to each animal, on whatever schedule you set.
  • Review what's coming up. Your task list and breeding calendar surface due litters, follow-ups, and to-dos so nothing slips.
5

Offline use

Creating an account and signing in require an internet connection. After you sign in, HerdMaster supports offline use. Public Everbreed pedigree-link imports require an internet connection.

iCloud sync: invitation-only limited pilotiCloud sync is currently available only to approved participants in a limited pilot who also have a Breeder or Show Breeder plan. Purchasing a plan does not grant pilot access.

That's the whole setup.
