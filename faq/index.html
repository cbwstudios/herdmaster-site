Getting started.

HerdMaster is a rabbitry and cavy management app for serious breeders: pedigrees, breeding records, litters, show tracking, and sales in one place. Built by a 45-year ARBA breeder and Best in Show winner, for breeders who take their programs seriously.

iPhone, on iOS 17 or newer.

Download HerdMaster from the App Store, create an account, then add your animals or import your herd from Evans or Everbreed. Your records are stored on your device.

Yes, and it's the full app, not a time-limited trial. The free Hobby tier is capped at up to 10 active herd animals, plus limits on customers and sale records, and includes Evans import so you can bring your herd in before deciding to upgrade.

Imports & migration.

Evans XML (the primary and recommended import path), Evans HTM and Evans text-based PDF pedigree import as fallbacks for older exports, Everbreed Animals CSV and Litters CSV on a Breeder or Show Breeder plan, a public Everbreed pedigree link, and manual entry.

An Evans XML import brings animals, winnings, and litters across in one pass and fills pedigrees showing the animal plus three ancestor generations, so you're not retyping ancestors.

HerdMaster imports Everbreed Animals CSV and Litters CSV exports for rabbits and cavies. Select the species before importing. Both CSV imports require a Breeder or Show Breeder plan. We recommend importing Animals CSV first, then Litters CSV. A litter row imports only when its sire and dam uniquely match existing animal records; rows with missing or ambiguous parent matches are skipped.

With an internet connection and a valid public Everbreed pedigree link, you can import one subject animal into Herd, with the ancestors visible on that pedigree saved as inactive, pedigree-only records. This works on any plan within its normal animal limits. The link does not import unrelated herd animals, litters, breeding history, health records, or tasks. These import options do not provide complete Everbreed account migration.

Offline & privacy.

Creating an account and signing in require an internet connection. After you sign in, HerdMaster supports offline use. Public Everbreed pedigree-link imports require an internet connection.

Billing & support.

Hobby is free with up to 10 active herd animals. Breeder is $7.99/month, $74.99/year, or $249.99 lifetime. Show Breeder is $9.99/month, $94.99/year, or $299.99 lifetime.

One time, nonrenewing purchase. Lifetime means access to the selected HerdMaster tier for the lifetime of the HerdMaster product or service. It does not mean your lifetime and does not guarantee that HerdMaster will operate indefinitely.

There's a free tier rather than a trial, capped but with no time limit, so you can use HerdMaster free for as long as you like within the caps.

You send payment requests through Venmo or PayPal and record the status yourself. HerdMaster does not process cards in-app.
